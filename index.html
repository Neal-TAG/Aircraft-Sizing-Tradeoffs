Payload and range

Drag against speed

Parasite drag rises with speed squared. Induced drag falls with speed squared. Weight lifts the induced curve; wing area lifts the parasite curve and moves the stall line. Speeds are equivalent airspeed, so the curves don't move with altitude. Both axes scale to the current aircraft.

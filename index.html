Payload and range

Range at constant altitude and constant equivalent airspeed, after taxi, climb and descent fuel and a timed reserve. The corners are: maximum payload; maximum payload with fuel up to maximum takeoff weight; full tanks with payload traded away; full tanks with no payload.

Drag against speed

Parasite drag rises with speed squared. Induced drag falls with speed squared. Weight lifts the induced curve; wing area lifts the parasite curve and moves the stall line. Speeds are equivalent airspeed, so the curves don't move with altitude. Both axes scale to the current aircraft.
